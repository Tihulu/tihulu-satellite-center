## What
`recording_dir_cdp` and `edit_job_cdp` had no CI coverage. They are now run in `interactive-removal-ui`, which already installs a headless browser and Node.

This closes a coverage gap - a regression in the pass-card recording folder or in editing a pending job would not have been caught by CI - and it gives the TLE-dependent suite a verification path on the runner network, because CelesTrak is currently returning 403 to this machine after the requests this session made (verified directly, and after ~25 minutes of backoff).
